---

A workflow can pause a contribution for a maintainer decision. Open **Human review** in the repository and select the item to see why the workflow stopped.

![The Human review queue shows why each contribution is waiting and offers Block and Approve actions.](/screenshots/human-review.png)

Approve the contribution to continue the workflow, or block it. Use **Activity** to open the run and inspect the recorded outcome.
